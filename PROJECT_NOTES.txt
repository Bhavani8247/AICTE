This project was built from the supplied AICTE/IBM SkillsBuild Masterclass 1 practice dataset and Masterclass 1–3 training materials. The Masterclass 3 workbook contains conflicting churn definitions (90 vs 180 days); the project documents and uses a leakage-safe 90-day forward target because the supplied data ends on 8 Dec 2026 and cannot support a full 180-day future window.
